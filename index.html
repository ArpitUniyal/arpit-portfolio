LearnHub

Full stack · AI · Education
AI-powered study material, made interactive.

A full-stack learning platform that turns uploaded PDFs into short notes, formulas, flashcards, and MCQ quizzes—so students can spend less time searching and more time understanding.

How it works

After secure sign-in, users upload a study PDF. Its text is extracted, processed in chunks, and sent through a multi-providerAI pipeline for structured learning-content generation. Gemini 3.5Flash-Lite is the primary model, with Groq GPT-OSS-20B as a secondary fallback and OpenRouter Free as the final fallback.

Key features
  • PDF upload & chunk-based processing
  • AI notes, formulas, flashcards & quizzes
  • JWT authentication & user-specific content
  • • Multi-provider AI pipeline with automatic fallback

Stack React · Node.js · Express.js · MySQL · Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite · Groq · OpenRouter · JWT

02

Credit Card Fraud Detection

Machine learning · Analytics
Finding rare fraud in imbalanced data.

A Random Forest model and Streamlit dashboard built to identify potentially fraudulent transactions from a highly imbalanced dataset of 284,807 records—only 492 of which were fraud.

0%Accuracy 0%Precision 0%Recall 0%F1-score

The model was evaluated with precision, recall, and F1-score, then tuned to a 0.30 probability threshold for a better balance between fraud detection and false positives. The dashboard supports batch CSV predictions, individual transaction analysis, probability visualisation, and model evaluation.

Stack Python · Scikit-learn · Random Forest · Streamlit
